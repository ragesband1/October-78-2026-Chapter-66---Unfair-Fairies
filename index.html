Story

Once upon a time in the heavenly castle of the fairies, there lived a proud group of fairies who mocked a humble fairy because he had no wings. They bullied him and called him weak. But the Enchanted Mother of the Fairies saw their unfairness and cursed them, stripping away their pride and testing their nature.


The humble fairy remained gentle and fair, while the prideful fairies became jealous and spiteful. When a poor man named Gikag cried out for help, the proud fairies failed to grant his wish because they were selfish and unfair. Yet the humble fairy answered with compassion and true magic.


Moral lesson: when we are different, we must remain humble, fair, and honest. Jealousy and greed only lead to pain. A true heart is stronger than wings.


The fairies who were filled with envy were punished, but the humble fairy was rewarded because he chose fairness over pride. In the end, hope and kindness win over envy and unfairness.
